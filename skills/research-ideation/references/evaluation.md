# Evaluation rubric

Use these engineering defaults as a synthesis of the literature. They are not a
reproduced benchmark or a calibrated reward model.

## Hard gates

Reject or leave provisional when a central source is invented/unread; a methodological
gap is supported only by abstracts; a mechanism duplicates ordinary solutions; assumptions
violate the contract; the validation leaks evaluation information or reasons circularly; or no observation,
proof obligation, or counterexample can test the claim.
Do not average away a failed gate.

## Ordinal scores, 0–4

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| novelty | duplicate or unassessed | meaningful adaptation | consequential non-obvious operation beyond retrieved work |
| grounding | unsupported | partial evidence with boundaries | gap and mechanism traced to inspected evidence |
| soundness | contradiction | plausible, unresolved assumptions | coherent causal argument, alternatives addressed |
| feasibility | violates contract | uncertain, pilot possible | implementable within documented resources |
| testability | unfalsifiable | measurable, weak discrimination | minimal test distinguishes strong alternatives |

Use 1 and 3 between anchors. Explain scores in prose. A 4 never establishes global
novelty or guaranteed correctness.

## Non-obviousness check

Derive ordinary solutions before seeing ideas. Freeze them. Compare exact technical
operations and identify the missing consequential step, or classify direct reuse,
routine extension, or unassessed. If ordinary derivation failed, retry or leave
unassessed; a weak comparison baseline is not novelty evidence.

A single-agent split is procedural, not truly blind. The lock checks consistency only.

## Selection and evidence

Compare anonymous candidates under the same contract and evidence ledger.
Review novelty, feasibility, mechanism clarity, and information gain separately.
Swap order on close cases and retain disagreement.
Select a Pareto portfolio; keep a feasible alternative where useful.

Specify falsification, suitable comparators, independent units, assumptions, uncertainty,
and stop rules before validation. In theory, compare exact claims and proof dependencies;
in observational studies, examine identification and confounding. Reviewer scores alone
do not establish scientific utility. Attach measured or proof artifacts before using
preliminary_validated or independently_validated status.
